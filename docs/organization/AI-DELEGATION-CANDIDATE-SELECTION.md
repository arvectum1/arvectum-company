## 1. Purpose

AC-601 selects the first real Company Position to be taken through the M6 AI-held Position proof.

The task does not invent a new Position or agent roster. It evaluates the six already approved Positions against actual Company workload and existing governance/evidence, then selects the smallest credible AI delegation candidate whose operational and economic case can be tested without broadening authority prematurely.

The output is a Position selection. It does not by itself create a new Principal, Assignment, runtime, credential, budget, customer commitment, AM-3/AM-4 authority or production approval. Those belong to later M6 work items.

## 2. Canonical evidence basis

The selection relies on current approved Company state, especially:

- AC-104 — Owner workload, manual work and bottleneck map;
- AC-202 — Reserved Owner Decisions;
- AC-203 — delegated Position authority, approval and escalation model;
- AC-204 — Initial Position Registry;
- AC-205 — Initial Assignments and executor classification;
- AC-206 — Company data/tool/credential access boundary baseline;
- AC-207 — continuity, replacement and manual fallback baseline;
- AC-501…AC-507 — first governed workflow evidence and economic direction;
- current active product/repository work that already uses AI/software for engineering execution.

The analysis distinguishes fact from hypothesis. Exact hours, utilization percentages and monetary savings remain unknown unless observed; they are not fabricated for selection.

## 3. Selection criteria

Candidates are assessed against eight bounded criteria:

1. **Real workload exists now** — repeated work is already occurring and does not need to be invented to justify the Position.
2. **Owner burden reduction potential** — delegation can remove execution/coordination load while preserving Owner control.
3. **AI execution fit** — substantial work is legible, tool-mediated, testable and decomposable for AI/software execution.
4. **Bounded authority feasibility** — useful work can be performed inside existing AM-0/AM-1/AM-2 boundaries without requiring AM-3/AM-4 or reserved decisions.
5. **Evidence quality** — outputs can be checked through repositories, tests, diffs, CI, artifacts or other attributable evidence.
6. **Reversibility and fallback** — runtime/agent replacement and human/manual fallback are practical.
7. **Data/access fit** — useful execution can proceed with least privilege and without default access to raw reusable secrets, bank/signing or unrelated customer systems.
8. **Economic testability** — M6 can measure owner time, engineering throughput/rework, runtime/tool cost and quality without pretending profitability is already proven.

A candidate with high theoretical leverage but weak current workload, poor evidence or high consequential authority is not preferred as the first proof.

## 5. Selected candidate

AC-601 selects:

**`POS-004 — Engineering & Release Lead`**

as the first Position for the M6 AI-held Position proof.

This selection is based on current evidence, not a claim that engineering is the Company's most important function in the abstract.

## 6. Why POS-004 wins

### 6.1 Real workload already exists

AC-104 already records that substantial coding can be performed by AI/software while the Owner still initiates, sequences, reviews and coordinates work. It also identifies work sequencing/repository orchestration, routine task decomposition, QA/regression/packaging, status synchronization and local execution gates as material sources of Owner execution or coordination burden.

The workload therefore exists independently of M6. AC-601 is not creating fake headcount to validate an architecture.

### 6.2 The Position is already intentionally AI-led

AC-205 explicitly classifies `POS-004` as `AI-led`: AI is the primary executor for admitted bounded technical work, with escalation at scope/customer/risk/access/external-effect/ROD boundaries.

M6 can therefore convert an existing executor pattern into a persistent accountable Principal/Assignment/runtime proof rather than reversing the approved operating model.

### 6.3 Existing access ceiling is unusually suitable

AC-206 already allows meaningful bounded repository/worktree/build/test execution access for `POS-004`, while denying default organization-wide administration, raw reusable secret access, bank/signing/commercial privilege and ambient production/customer-system access.

This creates a practical least-privilege pilot surface.

### 6.4 Engineering produces strong objective evidence

For POS-004, M6 can evaluate concrete evidence such as:

- accepted task scope and repository ownership;
- branch/worktree state;
- implementation diff;
- tests and regression results;
- CI/build/package evidence;
- review findings;
- defects/rework;
- completion/escalation correctness;
- owner intervention count/minutes;
- runtime/tool cost where actually observable.

This evidence is stronger for a first AI-held Position than customer persuasion quality, strategic judgment or risk acceptance, which are harder to evaluate cleanly without consequential ambiguity.

### 6.5 Replacement is conceptually supported

AC-207 already establishes that a specific model/agent/runtime is replaceable for POS-004 and that runtime replacement does not transfer Organizational Authority. Actual runtime-swap evidence remains to be proven later, which makes it a suitable M6 test rather than a solved assumption.

### 6.6 Economic hypothesis is credible but still unproven

The bounded economic hypothesis is:

> If a persistent AI Principal can own routine engineering execution and evidence preparation across admitted tasks, Owner attention should move from task scheduling and low-risk engineering supervision toward exceptions, customer value, product judgment and reserved decisions, while maintaining or improving engineering quality and reducing coordination/reconstruction cost.

This is a hypothesis for AC-602/AC-605 measurement, not a current profitability claim.

## 7. Why POS-002 is second, not first

`POS-002 — Commercial & Customer Lead` has high potential value because AC-104 identifies customer-context continuity, feedback/exception handling, intake normalization, support triage and draft customer updates as real bottlenecks.

However the first proof should avoid unnecessary ambiguity between drafting, recommendation, external effect, customer acceptance and material commitment. POS-002 is therefore retained as the strongest next AI-held candidate after the Company proves a persistent Position pattern in the safer and more measurable POS-004 contour.

This does not prevent current hybrid AI assistance under POS-002.

## 8. Why POS-003 is not first

`POS-003 — Portfolio & Product Lead` is well suited to AI synthesis, evidence assembly and option preparation, but final start/stop, major investment, priority and Company↔Product↔OS boundary decisions can enter ROD-04/ROD-09 and related Owner-reserved territory.

The first AI-held Position should demonstrate durable execution before testing a Position where the highest-value decisions are more judgment-heavy and authority-sensitive.

## 9. Authority and non-effect boundary

AC-601 selects a Position only.

It does **not**:

- create `AI-ENG-001` or another Principal identity;
- amend the meaning of POS-004;
- broaden AC-205 Assignment authority;
- activate AM-3 or AM-4;
- delegate any ROD-01…ROD-09 final decision;
- provision GitHub, GitVerse, CI, cloud, production, customer-system or secret access;
- authorize autonomous merge/release/deploy/external effects beyond current approved workflow/product boundaries;
- create new recurring spend or vendor dependence;
- make a model/runtime the Position itself;
- close M5;
- prove M6 operationally or economically complete.

The effective executable scope remains:

```text
Position authority envelope
∩ Assignment scope
∩ technical authorization/access
∩ current workflow/data/risk conditions
= maximum executable action
```

## 10. M5 / M6 sequencing reconciliation

Roadmap 0.44.0 stated that M6 should begin only when M5 evidence is sufficient to identify a real delegation candidate rather than invented demand.

The current Owner instruction explicitly re-admits AC-601 as a bounded selection task while M5 remains open. This is acceptable because:

1. AC-601 has no customer/external effect;
2. it changes no authority or access;
3. the real POS-004 workload is independently evidenced by AC-104/AC-205 and current product work;
4. the selection is reversible;
5. M5 remains open and its missing customer evidence is not fabricated or bypassed.

This early bounded admission does **not** automatically authorize AC-602…AC-607. Each later step must preserve current authority, budget, data and risk gates.
