## Objective

Establish the smallest measurable business case for proving a persistent AI-held POS-004 without fabricating utilization, savings or profitability.

## Real workload included

The pilot includes only recurring engineering work that already exists in current Company/product execution: repository-state inspection, bounded task implementation, routine technical decomposition implied by an accepted objective, code/config/doc changes inside declared path scope, declared tests/regression/build execution, git/test/log evidence collection, and failure/escalation classification.

It excludes customer communication/acceptance, product-scope creation, portfolio decisions, Company↔Product↔OS boundary changes, material dependency/spend decisions, signing/payment, production/customer-system actions and raw reusable-secret handling.

## Owner burden hypothesis

Current Company evidence already shows that AI/software can perform substantial engineering execution while Owner attention remains involved in initiating, sequencing, interpreting technical reports and relaying intermediate instructions between planning and coding tools.

Hypothesis: a persistent bounded runner/Principal can reduce this coordination and reconstruction burden while preserving Owner control at promotion and protected decision gates.

## Measurement model

No defensible historical timesheet or monetary baseline exists, so the pilot records evidence rather than invented values. For each real task, measure where observable: elapsed runner time, Owner interventions before `READY_FOR_OWNER`, review/promotion actions, executor attempts, failed checks, tests/pass-fail results, path-policy violations, rework after review, provider/runtime cost where available, and whether the task completed without the Owner manually relaying intermediate instructions.

Unknown cost remains unknown rather than zero.

## Continue / narrow / stop

Continue if several real tasks demonstrate correct fail-closed behavior, reproducible reviewable evidence, acceptable rework, fewer intermediate Owner coordination actions, plausible tool cost relative to avoided coordination/rework, and no need for ambient privileged access.

Narrow or stop if routine tasks repeatedly require Owner interpretation, produce unreviewable large diffs, bypass boundaries, create more rework than they save, or need broad secrets/admin/production access to be useful.

`AC-602 — Complete / PASS for bounded pilot baseline`.
